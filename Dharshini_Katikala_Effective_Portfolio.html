A practical foundation in cloud data engineering, supported by project work and a collaborative mindset.

Hello! I'm Dharshini Katikala, an Electronics and Communication Engineering graduate with internship experience as a GCP Data Engineering Intern at SignerTech IT Solutions Pvt. Ltd. I enjoy solving data challenges, learning new tools, and contributing to reliable data solutions.
